Bundled third-party libraries for PMCL legacy translation (RetroWrapper).

These jars are shipped inside PMCL and extracted at launch time into
~/.pmcl/libraries/retrowrapper/<version>/ when needed.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RetroWrapper 1.7.8 (NeRdTheNed fork)
  Source: https://github.com/NeRdTheNed/RetroWrapper
  License: MIT
  File: RetroWrapper-1.7.8.jar

LegacyLauncher / LaunchWrapper (Java 9+)
  Source: https://github.com/LightWayUp/LegacyLauncher
  File: launchwrapper-1.13-java-9-and-above.jar

FrankenLWJGL (universal macOS LWJGL 2 natives)
  Source: https://github.com/NeRdTheNed/FrankenLWJGL
  License: CC0 (script); LWJGL natives under LWJGL license
  File: lwjgl-platform-franken-osx.jar

LWJGL 2.9.4-nightly-20150209 (matching Franken natives; fixes black Display on modern macOS)
  Source: https://libraries.minecraft.net/org/lwjgl/lwjgl/
  Files: lwjgl-2.9.4-nightly-20150209.jar, lwjgl_util-2.9.4-nightly-20150209.jar

jinput-m1 (Apple Silicon jinput natives)
  Source: https://github.com/r58Playz/jinput-m1
  File: jinput-platform-m1-osx.jar

OW2 ASM 9.7
  Source: https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/org/ow2/asm/
  License: BSD-3-Clause
  Files: asm-9.7.jar, asm-commons-9.7.jar, asm-tree-9.7.jar,
         asm-analysis-9.7.jar, asm-util-9.7.jar

JOpt Simple 5.0.4
  Source: https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/net/sf/jopt-simple/
  License: MIT
  File: jopt-simple-5.0.4.jar
